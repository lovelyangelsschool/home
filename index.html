About Us

Building Character, Inspiring Minds

Since 2053 B.S., Lovely Angels School has been a benchmark for quality education in Nepal, fostering academic brilliance and ethical values.

Our Vision

To be a global standard institution that nurtures creative, confident, and compassionate world citizens.

Our Mission

Empower students with 21st-century skills, moral integrity, and academic excellence through innovative learning.

Core Values

Respect, Integrity, Excellence, Innovation, and Community Service — pillars of our daily practice.

"Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world." — Nelson Mandela

Principal's Message: "At Lovely Angels, we focus on every child's potential and craft an environment where curiosity thrives." — Mr. Manoj Regmi
